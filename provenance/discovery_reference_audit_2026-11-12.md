# Citation and reference audit — v0.2

Checked November 12, 2026. The bibliography contains 25 entries; every entry is cited in the manuscript. This audit separates bibliographic verification from checking the particular result used. It is not an exhaustive priority search or an independent audit of every theorem in the cited literature.

Publisher records, author-hosted texts, and arXiv versions were preferred. Some Project Euclid and DOI endpoints returned security challenges. Where a full original was not accessible, that limitation is stated below; a citation was not treated as proof of an assertion merely because its title appeared in a search result. No copyrighted source PDFs are redistributed in this bundle.

## Theorem-level dependencies newly clarified

**Principal representations.** Pinelis, Proposition 2(I), gives uniqueness and independence of the extremizers from the added target within its oriented Chebyshev class. Proposition 2(IIa) gives the endpoint support descriptions used here. The survey explicitly avoids the terms “canonical” and “principal representation,” but includes their relevant content. Karlin–Studden is cited as the classical monograph. The revised manuscript proves the real-rooted Hermite orientation separately, rather than asserting an endpoint sign from terminology. The PDF proposition was checked visually as well as in text.

**Chernoff minimax equality.** Sion, the one-compact-space corollary on printed p.174, applies to the affine-in-measure, convex-in-perspective objective. The noncompact positive half-line for the Chernoff parameter is permitted. The relevant page was visually checked. The manuscript does not claim attainment at a positive finite parameter, nor equality for a nonconvex integer-population class.

**Esty's variance.** Zhang–Zhang (2009), equations (1.3)–(1.5), reproduce Esty's fixed-sample normal calibration with the correction `-Phi_1^2/n^3`. Their equations (2.7)–(2.8) display the independent-Poisson centered-error variance. Those pages were checked in the journal reprint. Esty's original was blocked by the journal's security layer, so the manuscript explicitly says “as recorded and extended by Zhang and Zhang” rather than implying direct inspection of the entire 1983 proof.

**Eriksson's uniqueness.** Version 4, September 9, 2026, was checked. Proposition 11, Definition 12, and Theorem 14 are the precise reference chain: the synchronous-expeditions property and atemporality define the procedure class. The near-future uniqueness claim is not paraphrased as uniqueness among all label-symmetric estimators. Prediction and bounded-incidence results are acknowledged as prior art.

**Polyanskiy–Wu.** The published general-theory paper is now a separate 2026 reference: Annals of Statistics 54(1), 1–24, DOI `10.1214/25-AOS2498`. The official IMS issue record confirms metadata and its distinction from the companion applications. The combined arXiv preprint is retained for the unseen-population applications; it is not conflated with the published article.

## Citation scope

The paper does not claim novelty for general moment duality, principal representations, Markov/e-value calibration, concentration inequalities, Sion's theorem, or comparison principles. Specialized kernels, contact reductions and certificates are proved explicitly. A result generated by the framework is not automatically claimed new to the literature.

All citations resolve to bibliography keys in the standalone source. The build check verifies that there are no missing citations or cross-references. Public source links are for provenance; live availability can change.
